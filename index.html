Privacy Policy

Royal Board Games (Ludo & Chess) · Android package com.ludo.chess
Effective date: 8 October 2026

In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. It does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information. The only data that leaves your device is anonymous crash and diagnostic data, sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics so bugs can be found and fixed.

1. Who we are

Royal Board Games (“the app”) is developed and published by Yash Shah (“we”, “us”). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at yash054shah@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Crash and diagnostic data. If the app crashes or encounters an error, it uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to send a crash report. A report may include:

  • technical details of the error (stack trace and the state of the app when it happened);
  • device information, such as device model, operating system version, available memory and disk space, orientation, and whether the device is rooted;
  • app information, such as app version and the time of the crash;
  • a randomly generated installation identifier (Crashlytics installation UUID), which is used to count how many users are affected by a crash. It is not linked to your identity.

Crash reports do not contain your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or the content of your games.

Information that stays on your device. Your sound on/off setting is saved locally on your device so it is remembered between launches. Player names you enter during a game are used only within that game. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

3. How we use information

Crash and diagnostic data is used only to detect, diagnose and fix bugs and to improve the stability of the app. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build profiles of users, and we do not sell any data.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or trade any data. Crash and diagnostic data is processed on our behalf by our service provider:

ProviderPurposePrivacy information
Google LLC – Firebase Crashlytics Crash reporting and app stability Firebase privacy & security · Google Privacy Policy

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law.

5. Data retention

Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days and are then deleted automatically. Settings stored on your device remain until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

6. Security

Crash data is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS) and stored on Google’s secure infrastructure.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can delete all data stored on your device by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Because crash reports are not linked to your identity, we usually cannot identify which reports came from you. If you would like us to delete crash data or have any other privacy request, email yash054shah@gmail.com and we will help as far as we are able.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, or Indian data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to complain to a data protection authority.

8. Children’s privacy

The app is suitable for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The only data collected is the anonymous crash data described above. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Permissions

The app requests internet and network-state access only so that crash reports can be sent. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the app gains new features. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
